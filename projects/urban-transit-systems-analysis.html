Urban Transit Systems Analysis

Completed a research-focused systems analysis project examining transit systems in cities including London, Singapore, Tokyo, and Chicago.

Overview

Analyzed how technology, infrastructure, governance, and public policy influence transportation efficiency and modernization.

The project looked at transit as a large technical and operational system, connecting engineering choices with public needs, constraints, reliability, and modernization goals.

What It Shows

  • Research and analysis of large-scale systems.
  • Connection of technical concepts with operational challenges.
  • Understanding of infrastructure, policy, and modernization.

Skills Used

Research, systems analysis, infrastructure comparison, technical writing, public policy awareness, and analytical thinking.
